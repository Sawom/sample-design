School History

Over 40 Years of Academic Excellence & Community Leadership

Founded in 1985, Greenwood Model School started with just 50 students and a vision to make quality education accessible. Over four decades, it has grown into a premier institution housing over 2,500 students across Primary, Secondary, and Higher Secondary divisions.

Our commitment to moral values, innovative teaching, and athletic participation continues to mold compassionate, capable global citizens.
