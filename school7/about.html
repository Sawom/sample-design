Our Heritage & History

Established in 1985 by visionary educator Dr. Arthur Greenwood, Greenwood Model School originated as a single campus dedicated to holistic child development. Over four decades, the institution has continuously evolved, integrating digital smart classrooms, modern science laboratories, and extensive athletic grounds while preserving core moral values.

Headmistress's Speech

"Education is not merely the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think."

Welcome to Greenwood Model School. We believe every child harbors unique potential. Our dedicated educators nurture curiosity, resilience, and compassion, ensuring our graduates excel academically and contribute meaningfully to global society.

Our Mission

To deliver inclusive, high-quality education that inspires lifelong learning, critical thinking, ethical leadership, and active citizenship in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To remain a leading center of educational excellence, recognized for innovation, character building, and nurturing global leaders empowered with knowledge and empathy.
